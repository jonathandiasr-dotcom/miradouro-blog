A flat 16px-radius block. `dark` and `gold` tones flip the nested text tokens automatically.

```jsx
<Card tone="light" hoverable>
  <h3 className="mrt-heading">Cloud &amp; DevOps</h3>
  <p className="mrt-body">Audit organisationnel DevOps.</p>
</Card>
```

Never add a coloured left border; never stack two shadows. To decorate, crop a `<Wave>` into a corner.

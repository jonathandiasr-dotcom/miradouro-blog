The Meritis onde — quarter-disc or concentric quarter-arcs, cropped off the edge of a section, used as the single graphic accent.

```jsx
<Wave shape="arcs" color="gold" size={320} rotate={90} />
<Wave shape="pleine" color="blue" size={480} style={{position:'absolute',right:0,bottom:0}} />
```

Rules: principal palette colours only, 90° rotations only, never mix two colours in one onde, never change stroke weight or arc frequency, never overlay two colours — a white 10% onde over bleu marine is the one permitted superposition.
